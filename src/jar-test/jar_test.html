🫙 Jar test

Photograph your settled soil jar and we'll work out what is in it.

1. Your jar

  1. Fill a clear, straight-sided jar about one third with soil. Add water until nearly full, plus a squirt of dish soap.
  2. Shake well for a couple of minutes, then leave it on a flat surface.
  3. Wait 24–48 hours so the clay settles, then take a photo in good, even light with the jar upright and the camera level with the soil.

Drag a box from just below the lid down to the bottom of the soil. Keep it inside the glass and away from labels and bright reflections. We'll find the layers. Drag a line to move it.
